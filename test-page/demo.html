Adoption policy

Pursuant to the shelter's operational guidelines, prospective adopters are required to furnish documentary evidence of residential tenancy or ownership, accompanied by written authorisation from the landlord where applicable, prior to the commencement of the adoption assessment procedure. Applicants must additionally demonstrate the availability of adequate financial resources for the ongoing provision of veterinary care, nutrition and associated expenses, and must acknowledge that the shelter reserves the right, at its sole discretion, to decline any application without the obligation to furnish reasons, notwithstanding any prior communication that may have been construed as indicative of approval.
