Riverside Council

Residents' parking rules

This document sets out the terms under which the Council issues and administers residents' parking permits within the controlled parking zones designated under the Riverside (On-Street Parking) Order. Applicants are advised to consult the Order in its entirety prior to the submission of any application, and the Council shall not be held responsible for any loss arising from a failure to do so.

1. Who can apply

An application may be made by any person whose sole or principal residence is situated within a controlled parking zone, provided that such person is able to furnish two items of documentary evidence dated within the preceding three months, each bearing the applicant's full name and the address in respect of which the application is made. A maximum of two permits shall be issued in respect of any single household, save where the Council is satisfied that exceptional circumstances subsist.

Applicants residing in developments designated as car-free by virtue of a planning obligation shall not be eligible for a permit, irrespective of the duration of their occupation or any representation made by a vendor, landlord or letting agent. Students residing in term-time accommodation may apply for a permit of limited duration, which shall expire on the final day of the academic year to which it relates.

2. Which vehicles

Permits shall be issued solely in respect of passenger vehicles, motorcycles and goods vehicles whose overall height does not exceed 2.3 metres and whose overall length does not exceed 5.25 metres. The vehicle must be registered to the applicant at the address stated in the application, or, where the vehicle is provided by an employer, a letter on headed paper confirming the arrangement must be supplied. Trailers, caravans and motor homes are excluded without exception.

3. Charges

The charge for a first permit is 64 pounds per annum and the charge for a second permit is 118 pounds per annum. Vehicles with recorded emissions of 75 grams per kilometre or less attract a reduction of fifty per cent upon the first permit only. Blue Badge holders are exempt from all charges upon production of a valid badge. Charges are payable in full at the time of application, and instalment arrangements are not available.

Where a permit is surrendered prior to its expiry, a refund shall be calculated by reference to the number of complete calendar months remaining, less an administrative deduction of 12 pounds. No refund shall be payable in respect of any period of less than three complete months, nor in respect of any permit which has been withdrawn by the Council.

4. How to apply

Applications shall be submitted by means of the online form, by post, or in person at the Civic Hall. The Council shall endeavour to determine applications within ten working days of receipt of all requisite documentation. Incomplete applications shall be retained for a period of twenty-eight days, following which they shall be treated as withdrawn and any documents supplied shall be destroyed by secure means.

A temporary permit, valid for a period of fourteen days, may be issued to an applicant who has recently taken up residence and is not yet in possession of the documentary evidence referred to in section 1. No more than one temporary permit shall be issued to any household within a period of twelve months.

5. Visitors

Each household is entitled to purchase up to forty visitor vouchers in any permit year, at a charge of 1 pound 80 pence per voucher, each valid for a single calendar day. Households comprising one or more persons aged seventy-five years or over, or in receipt of care provided at home, are entitled to a further sixty vouchers without charge. Vouchers must be activated by entering the visitor's registration mark prior to 10 am on the day of use, or within fifteen minutes of arrival where arrival occurs subsequently.

6. Conditions of use

Possession of a permit does not guarantee the availability of a parking space, nor does it confer any entitlement to park in a bay reserved for another purpose, including bays designated for disabled persons, doctors, car clubs or electric vehicle charging. Permit holders must notify the Council of any change of vehicle or address within seven days. A vehicle displaying no valid electronic permit may be issued with a penalty charge notice, notwithstanding that an application is pending.

The Council may suspend any bay at such notice as is reasonably practicable for the purposes of highway works, removals, filming or public events. Where a bay is suspended, permit holders may park in any other residents' bay within the same zone. The Council accepts no liability for the cost of removal where a vehicle remains in a suspended bay after the commencement of the suspension.

7. Withdrawal and review

The Council may withdraw a permit where it has been obtained by misrepresentation, transferred for value, or used in respect of a vehicle other than that to which it relates. Prior to withdrawal, the permit holder shall be afforded fourteen days within which to make written representations. A person aggrieved by a refusal or withdrawal may request a review within twenty-one days of the date of the decision, which shall be conducted by an officer who took no part in the original determination.
